## Code ≠ Brand

Licensing of the source code does **not** automatically license the brand.
The following are **not** covered by the Apache-2.0 license:

* the name "Cardinal";
* the Cardinal logo;
* branding and visual identity;
* official product names ("Official Cardinal", "Official Cardinal Pro",
  "Official Cardinal Enterprise").

The Apache License itself (Section 6) does not grant permission to use
trade names, trademarks, service marks, or product names of the Licensor,
except as required for reasonable and customary use in describing the
origin of the Work.

## Fair use

Honest, non-misleading formulations are allowed without separate permission:

* "based on Cardinal";
* "forked from Cardinal";
* "compatible with Cardinal".

## Not allowed without permission

Do not present a third-party fork or derivative as an official Cardinal
product. In particular, do not call an unofficial project:

* "Official Cardinal";
* "Official Cardinal Pro";
* "Official Cardinal Enterprise";

unless you have separate written permission from the project maintainer.

It only clarifies how the Cardinal brand may be referenced.
