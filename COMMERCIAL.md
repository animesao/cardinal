## Cardinal Community

The open-source edition of Cardinal, licensed under the Apache License,
Version 2.0 (see LICENSE).

The following are allowed under Apache-2.0, with no payment required:

* use;
* modify;
* fork;
* redistribute;
* commercial use;
* derivative works.

Nothing in this document makes Apache-2.0 code paid for commercial use.
Commercial use of the open-source code remains free.

## Cardinal Pro

Future additional paid components or features.

If they are separate components distributed under their own explicit
commercial terms, they may be proprietary. Open-source code licensed
under Apache-2.0 is not affected.

## Cardinal Enterprise

Possible enterprise offerings:

* enterprise features;
* private deployments;
* enterprise support;
* SLA;
* custom integrations;
* consulting;
* custom development;
* migration;
* managed services.

## Cardinal Cloud / Hosted Services

A future paid hosted model is allowed.

Paid products, components, and services exist **on top of** the
open-source project. They do not retroactively restrict rights already
granted by the Apache License, Version 2.0.
